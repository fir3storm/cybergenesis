The problem

Security tools stop at detection: logs, alerts, dashboards, and human toil. Nobody proves a defense actually works before a real adversary tests it, and letting an AI change a firewall feels reckless — so automation stalls at recommendations while attackers adapt.

What you are watching

On a synthetic digital twin of an enterprise network, six agents run the full loop: the Observer turns telemetry into evidence, Muse Spark investigates and proposes (schema-validated, never executed directly), a deterministic policy engine — independent of the AI — approves, rejects, or escalates to a human, and the Verification Scientist replays the attack and produces an Ed25519-signed proof certificate. The Arena then pits red against blue on the same twin. A failed defense is reported as FAIL, never hidden.
