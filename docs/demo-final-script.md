# CYBERGENESIS — 3:00 demo narration

TTS: edge-tts, voice `en-US-GuyNeural`, rate -5%. Video: `docs/demo-final.mp4`, 180.000 s, 1440x810.
Live AI in the recorded take: Meta Muse Spark 1.3 (`meta/muse-spark-1.3`) served through Command Code.
Everything shown was built during the Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon (Oct 26 – Nov 4, 2026).

## 0:00 Problem (Welcome screen, Judge Mode)
Built entirely during the Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon. Security tools
stop at detection: alerts, but no proof a defense works, and an AI changing
firewalls feels reckless.

## 0:15 Solution (Living Twin)
CYBERGENESIS gives defenders a safe place to fight and prove: a living, fully
synthetic digital twin of an enterprise network, with every asset, link,
service and weakness. We can attack it as hard as we like.

## 0:33 Bring your own network (Twin Designer, Muse Spark vision)
You can bring your own network, too. Muse Spark's vision reads a network
diagram and turns it into a twin: assets, zones and links, validated against a
schema before anything is built. Same loop, your topology.

## 0:52 Attack and reasoning (Run Autonomous Defense Demo)
We launch a ransomware-style attack. The Observer turns telemetry into
structured evidence. Then Muse Spark, running live, investigates. Its
hypothesis cites the exact telemetry behind each claim, and separates what is
confirmed from what is speculation. The Strategist proposes a defense from the
attack graph alone, and every candidate is rehearsed on a cloned twin first.

## 1:20 Guardrails (policy gate, human approval)
But the AI never acts directly. A deterministic policy engine, with no language
model in it, validates every proposed action. High-impact defenses wait for a
human, and here the operator approves. Only allowlisted, typed actions can touch
the twin, and the AI has no shell, file system or network access.

## 1:46 Prove (Outcome, Verification Lab)
Now the attack is replayed independently, and the verdict here is PASS. A
failure would be shown as FAIL, never hidden. Every run exports an
Ed25519-signed certificate anyone can verify offline, and checks the fix
against the graph's minimum cut.

## 2:09 Arena, live (Arena: Play match, replay, decision evidence, held-out table)
The Arena puts both sides on the same twin, live. Muse Spark plays red, picking
an attack route from paths the engine has already validated, and plays blue,
picking a defense that must pass the same policy gate. If red still has a
route, it reroutes; if not, blue wins and gets a signed proof. Both moves here
come from Muse Spark, with its reasoning logged as evidence. Each match feeds
a score memory, never model weights, and every strategy is scored against the
provable optimum.

## 2:46 Close (Welcome)
CYBERGENESIS does not merely detect attacks. It tests defenses, proves they
work, and shows its evidence. Built on Muse Spark for the Meta Global
Hackathon.
